WHY IT IS PROBABLY NOT DERIVABLE. Vectric's Profile toolpath carries a per-contour start point
that the operator can place by hand. That lives in the .crv3d, not in the DXF - the same class
of missing information as the print jig's cut order. A DXF export re-emits a circle in

normalised away on export.

SOLVED WHILE INVESTIGATING THIS: why Vectric posts a circle as ~100 G1 moves rather than
G2/G3. The angular steps are NOT uniform - smallest (3.277 deg) exactly at 0/90/180/270 and
largest (3.799 deg) at the diagonals, 25 steps per quadrant. That is the fingerprint of a
RATIONAL QUADRATIC NURBS circle (four quarter segments, weights 1, cos45, 1) flattened at
uniform parameter steps; predicted vs measured agree element-wise to 0.008 deg across all 25.
So Vectric's circle is not an arc internally, and the offset of a NURBS is a NURBS - there is
no arc to emit. See ARC-FITTING.md.
