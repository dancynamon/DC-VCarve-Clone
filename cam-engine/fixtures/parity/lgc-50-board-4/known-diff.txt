1. Entry point 0.0018" out - ref (3.6712, 72.2940) vs ours (3.6720, 72.2924). Decomposed
   against the source's first segment, the reference entry sits 0.18996" PERPENDICULAR to the
   path (the tool radius, 0.1900, exactly right - confirmed independently by the tool database:
   T5e - Amana 49706 Roundover 0.380) and 0.00176" FORWARD along the tangent. So the offset
   distance is correct and the residual is purely where along the path Vectric's offset
   polyline starts. Its first chord direction implies a tangent of 116.42 deg where the
   source's first chord is 115.90 deg and a second-order one-sided estimate gives 115.38 -
   Vectric is evidently using the underlying spline, which the DXF does not give us.
   1.8 thou, on an entry into air, with a 0.380" cutter.
